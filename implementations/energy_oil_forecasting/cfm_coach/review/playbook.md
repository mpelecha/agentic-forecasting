# Coach playbook

Human-written until at least week 4; the coach reads it, never edits it.

## What counts
- The primary target is pinball against the random walk per horizon on live_forward rows. A proposal that does not move that, or coverage toward 80%, or direction above always-up, is a hypothesis, not a proposal.
- Numeric and policy proposals are priced by code on stored records. Say which fidelity: exact replay, approximate recombination, or a counterfactual action.
- LLM-judgment proposals need a pointer into pre-cutoff material for every supporting case. Hindsight text never reaches a proposal.
